# Safety and scope

RepairDB is an educational reference, not a repair manual, diagnosis, service bulletin, or substitute for the manufacturer's instructions or a qualified technician. The 42 records are illustrative examples. Naming an actual phone model does **not** mean that a listed symptom, component, or defect is known to occur on that model. Component lists describe generic possibilities, not diagnoses. Sample costs are illustrative USD parts-only ranges, not quotes or predictions.

## Stop and seek qualified help

Stop using and charging a device and seek qualified service if its battery is swollen, punctured, unusually hot, leaking, or emitting an unusual odor, smoke, or sound. Do not press, bend, puncture, heat, open, or attempt to remove a suspect battery. If a device is smoking or burning, do not handle it; move away and contact local emergency services as appropriate.

Do not charge a wet device or inspect it while connected to power. Liquid exposure, severe heat, smoke, damaged glass, or a device that behaves unpredictably can create risks beyond routine troubleshooting. Use the manufacturer's guidance and seek professional assessment.

## Keep checks non-invasive

Only try simple, reversible checks that are appropriate for the exact device and do not put you or the device at risk. Use compatible, reputable accessories. Do not insert metal tools, pins, or liquids into openings. Do not attempt improvised opening, board-level reflow, battery repair, bootloader unlocking, unofficial firmware, carrier-lock bypass, or IMEI/baseband alteration. Back up important data when possible; resets and recovery procedures can erase data.

A suggested check may not apply to every model, region, software version, or condition. Follow the exact manufacturer's manual and safety notices. If you are uncertain, stop and consult a qualified repair provider.

## Evidence and independence

RepairDB does not claim that its examples are verified model-specific faults. A future model-specific claim requires evidence scoped to the exact model, variant, and region, with a source, access date, and clear explanation of what the source establishes. General safety guidance is not evidence of a product defect. RepairDB is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with device manufacturers.
