SAFETY FIRST

Curiosity is good.
Know your limits.

Start with non-invasive checks. Stop if you see a swollen battery, smoke, liquid damage, broken glass, or excessive heat. Do not puncture lithium cells, attempt board-level reflow, or bypass baseband/carrier protections. For internal repairs, use the exact model’s official repair manual and qualified tools.
